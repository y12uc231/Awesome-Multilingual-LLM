## Foundational Multilingual Models & Analyses (pre-2023)

- `2022-12` **[Towards Leaving No Indic Language Behind: Building Monolingual Corpora, Benchmark and Models for Indic Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.693/)** — Doddapaneni et al. · *ACL 2023* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05409)]<br>IndicCorp (20.9B tokens, 24 Indic languages), the IndicXTREME benchmark (nine NLU tasks, 20 languages, 105 evaluation sets) and the IndicBERT v2 model.
- `2022-12` **[SERENGETI: Massively Multilingual Language Models for Africa](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.97/)** — Adebara et al. · *Findings of ACL 2023* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10785)]<br>SERENGETI: massively multilingual language model covering 517 African languages and varieties, outperforming four mPLMs on 11 of 20 datasets across eight NLU tasks.
- `2022-11` **[Speaking Multiple Languages Affects the Moral Bias of Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.134/)** — Hämmerl et al. · *Findings of ACL 2023* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.07733)]<br>Applies MoralDirection to multilingual LMs in German, Czech, Arabic, Chinese and English, finding they encode differing moral biases that do not match cultural differences in human opinions.
- `2022-07` **[No Language Left Behind: Scaling Human-Centered Machine Translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04672)** — Team et al.<br>NLLB: Mixture-of-Experts MT model trained on mined low-resource data, evaluated on over 40,000 directions with Flores-200 and a toxicity benchmark; improves BLEU 44% relative to prior work.
- `2022-07` **[Language Modelling with Pixels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.06991)** — Rust et al. · *ICLR 2022*<br>PIXEL: an 86M-parameter pretrained encoder that renders text as images, avoiding the vocabulary bottleneck; trained on English, it outperforms BERT on syntactic and semantic tasks in unseen scripts.
- `2022-05` **[The Geometry of Multilingual Language Model Representations](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.9/)** — Chang et al. · *EMNLP 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10964)]<br>Using XLM-R across 88 languages, shows languages occupy similar linear subspaces after mean-centering, with language-sensitive axes (e.g. vocabulary) orthogonal to language-neutral axes (position, part-of-speech).
- `2022-05` **[Same Neurons, Different Languages: Probing Morphosyntax in Multilingual Pre-trained Models](https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.114/)** — Stańczak et al. · *NAACL 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.02023)]<br>Neuron-level probing over 43 languages and 14 morphosyntactic categories finds significant cross-lingual neuron overlap, varying with category, language proximity and pretraining data size.
- `2022-05` **[Overcoming Catastrophic Forgetting in Zero-Shot Cross-Lingual Generation](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.630/)** — Vu et al. · *EMNLP 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12647)]<br>Shows multilingual generative models fine-tuned only on English summarization forget how to generate other languages; prompt tuning helps when transferring to less-related languages such as Thai.
- `2022-05` **[Analyzing the Mono- and Cross-Lingual Pretraining Dynamics of Multilingual Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.234/)** — Blevins et al. · *EMNLP 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11758)]<br>Probes XLM-R checkpoints across pretraining: in-language skills emerge early, cross-lingual transfer is learned at different times per language pair, and final-layer performance degrades over time.
- `2022-05` **[A Few Thousand Translations Go a Long Way! Leveraging Pre-trained Models for African News Translation](https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.223/)** — Adelani et al. · *NAACL 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.02022)]<br>Creates an African news translation corpus for 16 languages and shows fine-tuning large pretrained models on small amounts of high-quality translations works best for new languages and domains.
- `2022-04` **[Language Contamination Helps Explains the Cross-lingual Capabilities of English Pretrained Models](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.233/)** — Blevins & Zettlemoyer · *EMNLP 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08110)]<br>Shows English pretraining corpora contain significant non-English text, hundreds of millions of tokens even below 1%, which facilitates cross-lingual transfer of supposedly English-only models.
- `2022-04` **[Adapting Pre-trained Language Models to African Languages via Multilingual Adaptive Fine-Tuning](https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.382/)** — Alabi et al. · *COLING 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.06487)]<br>Multilingual adaptive fine-tuning of XLM-R and AfriBERTa on 17 African and three high-resource languages, removing non-African-script tokens to cut model size by about 50%.
- `2021-12` **[Few-shot Learning with Multilingual Generative Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.616/)** — Lin et al. · *EMNLP 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10668)]<br>Trains multilingual generative models whose largest, at 7.5B parameters, beats comparable GPT-3 on few-shot multilingual commonsense reasoning and NLI and on 171 of 182 FLORES-101 directions.
- `2021-10` **[When is BERT Multilingual? Isolating Crucial Ingredients for Cross-lingual Transfer](https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.264/)** — Deshpande et al. · *NAACL 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14782)]<br>Isolates factors in zero-shot cross-lingual transfer using modified language counterparts; missing subword overlap hurts transfer under word-order differences, and transfer correlates with embedding alignment.
- `2021-06` **[nmT5 - Is parallel data still relevant for pre-training massively multilingual language models?](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-short.87/)** — Kale et al. · *ACL 2021 (short)* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02171)]<br>nmT5: adds parallel data and MT objectives to mT5 pre-training, improving multilingual and cross-lingual tasks, with gains shrinking at larger model sizes except in limited-label settings.
- `2021-06` **[X-Fact: A New Benchmark Dataset for Multilingual Fact Checking](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-short.86/)** — Gupta & Srikumar · *ACL 2021 (short)* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09248)]<br>X-Fact: multilingual fact-checking dataset of real-world claims in 25 languages labeled by expert fact-checkers; the best multilingual transformer model reaches around 40% F-score.
- `2021-06` **[How to Adapt Your Pretrained Multilingual Model to 1600 Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.351/)** — Ebrahimi & Kann · *ACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02124)]<br>Adapts pretrained multilingual models to about 1600 languages using the New Testament; continued pretraining works best, with average gains up to 17.69% POS accuracy and 6.29 NER F1 over XLM-R.
- `2021-05` **[Larger-Scale Transformers for Multilingual Masked Language Modeling](https://aclanthology.org/2021.repl4nlp-1.4/)** — Goyal et al. · *REPL4NLP 2021 (workshop)* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.00572)]<br>Releases multilingual masked language models with 3.5B and 10.7B parameters that beat XLM-R by 1.8% and 2.4% XNLI accuracy and RoBERTa-Large on GLUE while handling 99 more languages.
- `2021-05` **[ByT5: Towards a Token-Free Future with Pre-trained Byte-to-Byte Models](https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.17/)** — Xue et al. · *TACL 2022* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.13626)]<br>ByT5: shows a standard Transformer processes raw bytes with minimal changes, competitive with token-level models and more robust to noise; releases pretrained byte-level T5 models.
- `2021-04` **[UC2: Universal Cross-lingual Cross-modal Vision-and-Language Pre-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00332)** — Zhou et al. · *CVPR 2021*<br>UC2: cross-lingual cross-modal pre-training that machine-translates English image captions and uses images as pivots, with new MRTM and VTLM objectives, evaluated on multilingual retrieval and VQA.
- `2021-03` **[Multilingual Multimodal Pre-training for Zero-Shot Cross-Lingual Transfer of Vision-Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.195/)** — Huang et al. · *NAACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.08849)]<br>Multi-HowTo100M multilingual video dataset and multimodal pretraining improve zero-shot non-English text-to-video search on VTT and multilingual search on VATEX and Multi30K.
- `2021-03` **[MuRIL: Multilingual Representations for Indian Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10730)** — Khanuja et al.<br>MuRIL: multilingual LM built for Indian languages, trained only on Indian-language corpora augmented with translated and transliterated document pairs as cross-lingual signals.
- `2021-01` **[Word Alignment by Fine-tuning Embeddings on Parallel Corpora](https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-main.181/)** — Dou & Neubig · *EACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08231)]<br>Fine-tunes multilingual pretrained LM embeddings on parallel text with alignment objectives, outperforming prior word aligners on five language pairs and enabling robust multilingual word aligners.
- `2021-01` **[First Align, then Predict: Understanding the Cross-Lingual Ability of Multilingual BERT](https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-main.189/)** — Muller et al. · *EACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.11109)]<br>Shows multilingual BERT acts as a multilingual encoder followed by a language-agnostic task predictor, with the encoder driving cross-lingual transfer, across three tasks and seventeen languages.
- `2021` **[Small Data? No Problem! Exploring the Viability of Pretrained Multilingual Language Models for Low-resourced Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2021.mrl-1.11/)** — Ogueji et al. · *MRL 2021 (workshop)*<br>AfriBERTa: multilingual model trained on under 1 GB of text in 11 African languages, competitive with or better than mBERT and XLM-R on NER and classification.
- `2020-12` **[UNKs Everywhere: Adapting Multilingual Language Models to New Scripts](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.800/)** — Pfeiffer et al. · *EMNLP 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15562)]<br>Adapts mBERT to low-resource languages and unseen scripts with matrix-factorization embedding methods that reuse lexically overlapping tokens, giving large gains for unseen scripts.
- `2020-12` **[How Good is Your Tokenizer? On the Monolingual Performance of Multilingual Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.243/)** — Rust et al. · *ACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15613)]<br>Compares multilingual and monolingual models on nine languages and five tasks; finds a dedicated monolingual tokenizer matters as much as pretraining data size for downstream performance.
- `2020-11` **[iNLPSuite: Monolingual Corpora, Evaluation Benchmarks and Pre-trained Multilingual Language Models for Indian Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.445/)** — Kakwani et al. · *Findings of EMNLP 2020*<br>iNLPSuite: 8.8 billion-token monolingual corpora, FastText embeddings, ALBERT models and the IndicGLUE benchmark for 11 major Indian languages.
- `2020-11` **[Identifying Elements Essential for BERT’s Multilinguality](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.358/)** — Dufter & Schütze · *EMNLP 2020*<br>Uses small BERT models on synthetic and natural data to identify four architectural and two linguistic elements that influence multilinguality, tested on XNLI.
- `2020-11` **[From Zero to Hero: On the Limitations of Zero-Shot Language Transfer with Multilingual Transformers](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.363/)** — Lauscher et al. · *EMNLP 2020*<br>Shows zero-shot transfer with mBERT and XLM-R weakens for distant and low-resource targets across POS, parsing, NER, NLI and QA, while few-shot target data is surprisingly effective.
- `2020-10` **[mT5: A Massively Multilingual Pre-trained Text-to-Text Transformer](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.41/)** — Xue et al. · *NAACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11934)]<br>mT5: multilingual T5 pretrained on a Common Crawl-based dataset covering 101 languages, with a technique to prevent accidental translation in zero-shot generation.
- `2020-10` **[WikiLingua: A New Benchmark Dataset for Cross-Lingual Abstractive Summarization](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.360/)** — Ladhak et al. · *Findings of EMNLP 2020* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03093)]<br>WikiLingua: cross-lingual abstractive summarization dataset of WikiHow article-summary pairs in 18 languages aligned via images, plus a direct cross-lingual summarization method.
- `2020-10` **[When Being Unseen from mBERT is just the Beginning: Handling New Languages With Multilingual Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.38/)** — Muller et al. · *NAACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12858)]<br>Shows multilingual models transfer poorly to some unseen languages largely because of their script, and that transliterating these languages significantly improves downstream performance.
- `2020-10` **[Rethinking embedding coupling in pre-trained language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12821)** — Chung et al. · *ICLR 2020*<br>Shows decoupling input and output embeddings lets multilingual models reallocate input-embedding parameters to Transformer layers, and larger output embeddings improve transfer, giving strong XTREME results without more fine-tuning parameters.

- `2020-07` **[InfoXLM: An Information-Theoretic Framework for Cross-Lingual Language Model Pre-Training](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.280/)** — Chi et al. · *NAACL 2021* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.07834)]<br>InfoXLM: frames cross-lingual pretraining as mutual-information maximization and adds a contrastive task on bilingual sentence pairs, using monolingual and parallel corpora to improve cross-lingual transfer.
- `2020-06` **[Pre-training via Paraphrasing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15020)** — Lewis et al. · *NeurIPS 2020*<br>MARGE: sequence-to-sequence model pretrained with an unsupervised multilingual multi-document paraphrasing objective, reaching up to 35.8 BLEU zero-shot on document translation.
- `2020-05` **[Finding Universal Grammatical Relations in Multilingual BERT](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.493/)** — Chi et al. · *ACL 2020* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.04511)]<br>Shows mBERT subspaces recover syntactic tree distances across languages, are approximately shared, and cluster dependency labels in ways largely matching Universal Dependencies.
- `2020-04` **[The State and Fate of Linguistic Diversity and Inclusion in the NLP World](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.560/)** — Joshi et al. · *ACL 2020* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09095)]<br>Quantifies disparities among the world's languages in resources and representation at NLP conferences, questioning the 'language agnostic' status of current models and systems.
- `2020-01` **[Multilingual Denoising Pre-training for Neural Machine Translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08210)** — Liu et al.<br>mBART: sequence-to-sequence denoising autoencoder pretrained on monolingual corpora in many languages, giving up to 12 BLEU gains in low-resource MT and enabling transfer to unseen pairs.
- `2019-12` **[Multilingual is not enough: BERT for Finnish](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.07076)** — Virtanen et al.<br>Compares multilingual BERT with a Finnish BERT trained from scratch; the language-specific model systematically outperforms mBERT on POS tagging, NER and dependency parsing.
- `2019-12` **[Cross-Lingual Ability of Multilingual BERT: An Empirical Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.07840)** — K et al. · *ICLR 2019*<br>Studies what drives M-BERT's cross-lingual ability on Spanish, Hindi and Russian; lexical overlap plays a negligible role while network depth is integral.
- `2019-11` **[Unsupervised Cross-lingual Representation Learning at Scale](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.747/)** — Conneau et al. · *ACL 2020* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.02116)]<br>XLM-R: masked language model pretrained on more than two terabytes of filtered CommonCrawl data in one hundred languages, outperforming mBERT on XNLI, MLQA and NER, especially for low-resource languages.

- `2019-09` **[Unicoder: A Universal Language Encoder by Pre-training with Multiple Cross-lingual Tasks](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1252/)** — Huang et al. · *EMNLP 2019* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.00964)]<br>Unicoder: cross-lingual encoder pretrained with cross-lingual word recovery, paraphrase classification and masked LM tasks, gaining 1.8% average accuracy over XLM on XNLI across 15 languages.
