## Multilingual Pretraining, Data Mixtures & Scaling

- `2026-09` **[Beetle: A Bilingual Model Suite for Modelling Second-Language Processing](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.2276/)** — Salhan et al. · *EMNLP 2026* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22633)]<br>Beetle: controlled pretraining framework releasing 285 bilingual and 45 monolingual LMs; staged, temporally structured curricula better match learner reading times than balanced bilingual training.
- `2026-08` **[One Form to Transfer Them All: Pretraining Multilingual Language Models Beyond Native Orthography](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1838/)** — Zhang et al. · *EMNLP 2026* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25904)]<br>Compares orthographic text, IPA and romanization for autoregressive multilingual pretraining at three scales on eight languages; romanized pretraining gives the strongest cross-lingual transfer, widening with scale.
- `2026-05` **[Scaling Laws for Mixture Pretraining Under Data Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12715)** — Sedova et al.<br>Across 2,000+ training runs including multilingual mixtures, finds scarce target corpora can be reused 15-20 times in mixture training and fits a repetition-aware mixture scaling law.
- `2026-01` **[The Role of Mixed-Language Documents for Multilingual Large Language Model Pretraining](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1706/)** — Shao et al. · *ACL 2026* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00364)]<br>Pretraining from scratch shows removing bilingual documents (2% of corpus) drops translation BLEU by 56% while cross-lingual QA and reasoning stay stable; parallel data restores 91%.
- `2026` **[ShapleyLaw: A Game-Theoretic Approach to Multilingual Scaling Laws](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1080/)** — Cao et al. · *EMNLP 2026*<br>ShapleyLaw: treats languages as players in a cooperative game to quantify cross-lingual transfer in multilingual scaling laws, improving loss prediction and language mixture optimization.
- `2026` **[M^3 Scaling Law: Optimizing Multi-Epoch, Multi-Lingual, and Multi-Stage Training for Low-Resource Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.2315/)** — Akimoto et al. · *Findings of EMNLP 2026*<br>M3 Scaling Law: models epochs, target-language ratio and multi-stage recipes for low-resource LLM pretraining with English plus Japanese, Indonesian or Swahili; two-stage multilingual training wins as data shrinks.
- `2026` **[ATLAS: Adaptive Transfer Scaling Laws for Multilingual Pretraining, Finetuning, and Decoding the Curse of Multilinguality](https://openreview.net/forum?id=0BkvUY61MX)** — Longpre et al. · *ICLR 2026*<br>ATLAS: multilingual scaling law from 774 training runs (10M–8B parameters, 400+ training languages, 48 evaluation languages), with a 38×38 cross-lingual transfer matrix and pretrain-versus-finetune crossover points.
- `2025-10` **[Revisiting Multilingual Data Mixtures in Language Model Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25947)** — Foroutan et al.<br>Trains 1.1B and 3B LLMs on 25 to 400 languages; finds English-multilingual mixing need not hurt either group, English pivots help across families, and no significant curse of multilinguality.
- `2025-09` **[Exploring Polyglot Harmony: On Multilingual Data Allocation for Large Language Models Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15556)** — Guo et al. · *NeurIPS 2025*<br>Climb: sets multilingual pretraining language proportions with a cross-lingual interaction-aware ratio and two-step optimization; LLMs trained with Climb proportions rival open LLMs trained on more tokens.
- `2025-06` **[Just Go Parallel: Improving the Multilingual Capabilities of Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1602/)** — Qorib et al. · *ACL 2025* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13044)]<br>Controlled experiments show that adding parallel data to LLM training significantly improves translation and multilingual commonsense reasoning.
- `2025-06` **[Group then Scale: Dynamic Mixture-of-Experts Multilingual Language Model](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.87/)** — Li et al. · *Findings of ACL 2025* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12388)]<br>Groups similar languages by per-layer parameter deviation and expands high-deviation layers into language-group mixture-of-experts, reducing negative transfer across 18 to 128 languages with fewer parameters.
- `2025-06` **[Assessing the Role of Data Quality in Training Bilingual Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1236/)** — Seto et al. · *Findings of EMNLP 2025* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12966)]<br>Finds unequal data quality, not just quantity, drives performance drops in bilingual models; filtering with only high-quality English data improves French, German and Chinese by 2-4%.
- `2025-05` **[M-Wanda: Improving One-Shot Pruning for Multilingual LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1369/)** — Choenni & Titov · *EMNLP 2025* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21171)]<br>Shows moderate sparsity already harms multilingual LLM performance and proposes M-Wanda, adding language-aware activation statistics and cross-lingual layerwise sparsity to one-shot pruning.
- `2025-04` **[Investigating and Scaling up Code-Switching for Multilingual Language Model Pre-Training](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.575/)** — Wang et al. · *Findings of ACL 2025* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01801)]<br>Finds code-switching in pretraining corpora key to multilingual ability, categorizes four types, and shows scaling synthetic code-switching data improves language alignment across high-, medium- and low-resource languages.
- `2024-11` **[Training Bilingual LMs with Data Constraints in the Targeted Language](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.977/)** — Seto et al. · *Findings of ACL 2025* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12986)]<br>Studies boosting a data-limited target language with auxiliary-language pretraining data; stronger auxiliary datasets help close languages, and better English pretraining data extends gains to the target language.
- `2024-10` **[Scaling Laws for Multilingual Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.221/)** — He et al. · *Findings of ACL 2025* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12883)]<br>Shows each language family's test loss depends only on its own sampling ratio and derives a power law over dataset size, model size and sampling ratios to set optimal mixtures.
- `2024-10` **[DEPT: Decoupled Embeddings for Pre-training Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05021)** — Iacob et al. · *ICLR 2024*<br>DEPT: decouples token embeddings from the transformer body to pretrain on heterogeneous sources without a shared vocabulary, targeting the curse of multilinguality; perplexity improves up to 20%.
- `2024-04` **[The Role of Language Imbalance in Cross-lingual Generalisation: Insights from Cloned Language Experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07982)** — Schäfer et al.<br>Cloned-language experiments show a dominant training language boosts less frequent languages and cross-lingual alignment; at scale a 90/10 bilingual split beats 50/50 on both languages.
- `2024-01` **[Breaking the Curse of Multilinguality with Cross-lingual Expert Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.604/)** — Blevins et al. · *EMNLP 2024* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10440)]<br>X-ELM: independently trains expert LMs on subsets of a multilingual corpus; at equal compute it beats jointly trained models on all 16 languages and adds languages without forgetting.
- `2023-12` **[Order Matters in the Presence of Dataset Imbalance for Multilingual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06134)** — Choi et al. · *NeurIPS 2023*<br>Shows that pre-training on high-resource tasks then fine-tuning on a high/low-resource mixture improves on static weighting under data imbalance, in NMT and multilingual language modeling.
- `2023-11` **[When Is Multilinguality a Curse? Language Modeling for 250 High- and Low-Resource Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.236/)** — Chang et al. · *EMNLP 2024* · [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09205)]<br>Pre-trains over 10,000 monolingual and multilingual LMs (up to 45M parameters) for 250+ languages; moderate multilingual data helps low-resource languages, but more data eventually hurts all (curse of multilinguality).
- `2023-05` **[Cross-Lingual Supervision improves Large Language Models Pre-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11778)** — Schioppa et al.<br>Shows mixing a supervised machine translation objective with language modeling in LLM pretraining improves in-context learning, and learns the mixing ratio during pretraining.
- `2023-04` **[UniMax: Fairer and more Effective Language Sampling for Large-Scale Multilingual Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09151)** — Chung et al. · *ICLR 2023*<br>UniMax: language sampling capping per-language corpus repeats, beating temperature sampling across scales; releases a refreshed mC4 of 29 trillion characters in 107 languages and umT5 checkpoints.
